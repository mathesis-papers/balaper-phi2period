# Polynomial-flow extensions of Su's theorem on Bala's period conjectures

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

We give an alternative proof and an extension of Lien-Hung Su's theorem (31 October 2026, Theorem 3.2 and Corollary 3.3, doi:10.5281/zenodo.23835577) resolving Peter Bala's conjecture in OEIS A281180, Comments, revision 86, signed 18 February 2026, and its four companion conjectures. The proof begins with a uniform operator identity for every integral polynomial vector field P(z)d/dz with P(0)=1. For the quadratic flows S'=C^a, C'=S C^(a-1), we extend the eventual period phi(m)^2/2 to every integer a>=1, every integral polynomial observable at even derivative indices, and every m>=3. We give explicit starting indices, prove residue-row and derivative identities from the neighboring entries, and supply exact finite-matrix certificates for the source examples.
